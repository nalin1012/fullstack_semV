AKATUSKI MEMBERS

Itachi Uchiha

Itachi was a talented shinobi and a member of the Akatsuki. He was known for his intelligence, powerful Sharingan, and calm personality.

Pain

Pain was the leader of the Akatsuki. He wanted to create peace through fear and the power of the Rinnegan.

Deidara

Deidara was an explosive artist who believed that true art should disappear in a beautiful explosion.

Kisame Hoshigaki

Kisame was a powerful swordsman who fought alongside Itachi. His sword, Samehada, absorbed chakra from enemies.

Sasori

Sasori was a skilled puppet master who turned human bodies into powerful puppets.

Konan

Konan was one of the founders of the Akatsuki and a skilled user of paper-based techniques. She was loyal to Yahiko and Nagato.

Zetsu

Zetsu served as a spy for the Akatsuki. He had two distinct personalities and was skilled at gathering information.

Tobi / Obito Uchiha

Obito operated under the identity of Tobi. He possessed the Sharingan and played an important role in the Akatsuki's plans.
